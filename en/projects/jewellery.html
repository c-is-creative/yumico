An early practice in making
1995–2003² · Spain / Germany / Japan
What I did
Spain: made by hand and learned within another culture. Hiko Mizuno: taught students while continuing an artistic practice.
The perspective that emerged
Spain: does value reside not only in uniformity, but also in people, land, culture, and handwork? Teaching: is there value not only in making oneself, but in enabling others to make?
Later applications / Developments
From Spain: international brands, FUEGUIA, PETALO? From teaching: education, NHUMA, PETALO?

² Study and teaching period. The date of this individual work is being established.
