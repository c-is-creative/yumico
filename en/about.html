About

Yumico Otsubo

Yumico Otsubo began with making. After moving from Japan to Spain in 1995, she studied metalsmithing, jewellery design, and gemology, later continuing her studies in Germany and Japan. Jewellery practice led to teaching, and to work across fashion, art, brands, places, and communities.

In 2023 she founded C’s CREATIVE and opened creative space NHUMA in Abiko. Her current work brings together branding, local resources, education, and opportunities for people to create and participate. She works across Japanese, English, and Spanish.
