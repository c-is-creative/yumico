# Editorial notes — prototype 03

The archive now includes all 12 practices from Yumico's supplied table, plus the earlier pictured entries. This produces 21 rows. Some are overlapping chapters of one period rather than independent commissions; the groupings are provisional.

The four card headings follow the supplied table. Their Japanese wording is preserved. English and Spanish versions are editorial drafts for review. Question marks remain wherever Yumico proposed a possible rather than established connection. For existing entries absent from the table, the activity is drawn from the supplied CV and the interpretation fields are shown as dashes. They should be completed through discussion, not inferred from a job description.

Text-only practices (H.P.FRANCE international brands, shop communication, WINDOW GALLERY and Ao-sando; i Kobo / ART COFFEE; Hiko Mizuno and Spain) are included now and await individual photo folders. The broader jewellery chapter remains visible as a visual bridge. The Marche photograph appears under Hajimari no Marche; NHUMA has the house views. A photograph can be used in more than one chapter when its relationship is explicit, but duplication should be reviewed with the later archive.

The background changed from beige to a near-white paper tone. Project titles use one serif face and size across collapsed and expanded rows. The header now leads directly into the index; an icon in the header links to the email address provided in the CV. Photo strips have equal heights, no CSS gaps, and slow movement only when content overflows. They can be paused and respect reduced-motion preferences.

Dates remain broad practice periods. The H.P.FRANCE 2003–2015 period is an employment span, not a claim about every specific project or photograph. The older jewellery span is a study and teaching period, not an object date. The table did not supply dates for the Marche or ART COFFEE, so these rows show a dash. For Next and TTC show their known start years without asserting continued activity. Confirm dates and image attributions before public launch.

Photographer and collaborator credits, especially for the Abiko illustration by ISNA DESIGN, need Yumico's review. The source CV also contains private details that are not included in the prototype. The email address is the sole contact item used for the requested contact icon.


Revision 03 removes the single-image sizing exception. Every strip follows the same height rule and checks overflow regardless of image count. Visible play/pause buttons are removed. Arrow and More controls share the row’s right-hand column. Main text and card content use the serif family; small uppercase card labels use faded sans-serif lettering with a subtle divider below each.
